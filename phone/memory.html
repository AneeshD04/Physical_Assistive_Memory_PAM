    Mark an episode

    1. Start the camera with the surface in view.
    2. Before moving an item, choose Mark before moving.
    3. Move the item, then choose Mark at rest & save.

    No hand landmarks or automatic recognition are invented. Marks are hints; the local processor may find only context, not an item. With automatic capture on, an episode starts when a hand looks busy and ends when the item rests; that is a heuristic, not verified recognition.
