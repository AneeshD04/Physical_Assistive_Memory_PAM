    Mark an episode

    1. Start the camera with the surface in view.
    2. Before moving an item, choose Mark before moving.
    3. Move the item, then choose Mark at rest & save.

    No hand landmarks or automatic recognition are invented. Marks are hints; the local processor may find only context, not an item. With automatic capture on, an episode starts when a hand looks busy and ends when the item rests; that is a heuristic, not verified recognition.

    Coverage & storage

    Keep this page visible and the screen unlocked. Backgrounding, locking, pauses and interruptions create coverage gaps. Pam does not promise all-day background capture.

    Unsynced episodes stay in this browser until the server confirms durable retention. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; it is not a backup. Use a trusted device.

    With automatic capture on, while nothing is happening and no hand is in view, one still is sent to the local service every 30 seconds so Pam can check whether items are still where they were. Those stills are not kept in this browser; one that fails to send is a coverage gap, not evidence.
