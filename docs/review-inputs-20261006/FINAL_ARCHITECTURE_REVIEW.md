# PAM memory layer: final architecture review before build

Date: 2026-10-06. Subject: `docs/MEMORY_SYSTEM_V4.md` revision 4 (commit `f7ca543`) and the code in `perception/`, `server/`, `phone/`.
Method: four reviewers (architecture, perception methods, memory/retrieval methods, commercialization) each wrote a full report, then a cross-review round in which every question between them was answered and every disagreement closed. The four full reports are in `docs/review/` and are the evidence for everything below; this document is the decision list. Claims about platforms, licenses and papers are marked in the full reports as verified-by-URL or belief; counsel confirms licenses before shipping. Nobody on the review is a lawyer.

## 1. Verdict

Build. The architecture holds: interaction-triggered capture, an event-sourced evidence ledger written from embeddings at episode time, photo-first answers with a first-class abstain, and no paid cloud on the write path. None of the four reviewers asked for a redesign, and the memory reviewer's survey of the field confirms that this "embed at write, structured ledger, photo at read" design is a third way nobody else benchmarks: the published alternatives either run a detector over all video at query time (Ego4D VQ2D family: 0.37 tAP25, about 40 s per query) or put a VLM on the write path (EgoLife, ReMEmbR, MemPal: 39% to 72% on object questions). The spec's 70% initial target sits inside what the field reaches, not above it.

Three things must change before the first line of build code:

1. **Eleven contract-level fixes go into the spec** (section 3). Nine were found by the architecture reviewer, two of which made scripted cases un-passable as written; two more came out of the cross-review (Safari landmark speed; hand geometry as a biometric surface). All eleven have an agreed fix.
2. **The repo is purged** (section 4). It carries AGPL and research-only artifacts and non-commercial footage that put a product company in breach today, not at ship time.
3. **Four spec numbers were wrong** and are replaced (section 8): the Safari hand-landmark estimate, the speech cost line, the per-user cloud cost, and the WebGPU version.

## 3. Spec changes required before day 1 (the contract freeze)

Each is a text change to `MEMORY_SYSTEM_V4.md`; none changes the architecture. Line numbers are revision 4.

| # | Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BL-1 | The Settled predicate (L182) depends on a "tracked object region" on the phone, but tier 3 (L114) says the phone has none | The phone controller is hand-only. Active to Settled when "hand busy" has been negative for 1.0 s and the hand has moved more than 1.5 hand-widths from where it went negative; or no hand for 1.0 s; or the 3 s gap. The phone emits `outcome_hint` in `released / hand_gone / gap`; the laptop localizer assigns `placed_on_surface / placed_in_container / lost_from_view / uncertain`. "Grasp" is renamed "hand busy" everywhere; it is a recall-tuned capture trigger, never called contact |
| BL-2 | `carry_flow` (L115) needs consecutive frames; the packet has 6 to 8 keyframes (L173) | Optional `carry_burst[]`: up to 10 consecutive analysis-resolution grayscale JPEGs at 10 fps centred on the carry, present only for carries of 0.5 s or more; the localizer returns `none` for `carry_flow` when spacing exceeds 150 ms. Usable LK spacing is about 100 ms; `CLAUDE.md` already records the failure at 333 ms |
| BL-3 | The pre-roll's rate is unspecified and the only number given (1 MB over 2 s) implies continuous 25 fps JPEG encoding | Pre-roll runs at the idle analysis rate (5 fps) rising to 10 fps in Escalating/Active; hold up to 10 `ImageBitmap`s in Idle and 20 in Active; encode to JPEG only on promotion, in the Worker via `OffscreenCanvas.convertToBlob` |
| BL-4 | Landmarks are defined in pixel coordinates of frames the server never receives | Landmarks and `hand_bbox` normalised to [0, 1] of the capture frame (MediaPipe's native output); the packet carries `capture{width,height}` and `analysis{width,height}` once. The analysis size (256 vs 384 px) is chosen by the R1 experiment, since the task resizes internally to 192/224 anyway |
| BL-5 | `identity_status = group` can never produce a reliable sighting, so case 4 forces `abstain` | A fourth answer shape `group`; the answer is restructured around `members[]` (0 for abstain, 1 for confident or hedged, 2..N for group), each member with its own photo, time, status, room and relevance; more than three members becomes `clarify` ("which one?") by voice; members keep separate crops, masks and OCR so a later Rx read splits the group |
| BL-6 | `placed_in_container` has no producing stage | The localizer gains `container_region {frame_id, bbox, item_id, confidence} | null` (geometry filled by the localizer; `item_id` and `is_container` filled by the identity stage). Minimum evidence for a containment link: no accepted rest region, the hand's release point inside a tracked item flagged `is_container`, and that item present in the rest frame. Automatic containment is beyond v1 (the best published tracker through occlusion reaches 16% IoU); case 5 is scored on ledger logic with hand-labelled containment events, and the spec says so |
| BL-7 | The tier-1 "cheap hand check" (L112) names no component, and no cheaper hand model exists | Tier 1 is non-neural: frame-difference energy over the lower 80% of the frame including both edges (wheelchair mounts see hands enter from the side), gating the landmarker, plus a 1 Hz heartbeat; `numHands = 1` outside Active; the skip rate is a week-1 counter |
| BL-8 | `stale` has no transition rule | `stale_reason` in `coverage_loss / negative_reobservation / age (12 h initial) / other_person_handling`, recorded on `item_current`; any reason yields `hedged`, never `abstain`; the speech names the reason |
| BL-9 | Nine smaller gaps that force guessing | Keyframe rule (`pre_contact` = newest pre-roll frame with no hand; 3 rest frames at 0.33 s); pause semantics (blur and head turns pause the Settled counter and append to `gaps[]`); `relevance` on every member; the re-observation stage as defined in section 2; `session_id` per page load and a deterministic `episode_id = base32(sha256(device_id | session_id | round(t_start_mono x 1000)))[:26]` with a clock anchor per packet; 4 MiB packet cap; the 0.5 s refractory applies to packet emission, not hand analysis; two rival diff regions become `target_regions[]` (max 2, `rival_of`); step 1 of section 10 is already done in code and its line reference is stale |
| BL-10 (new) | The spec's "20 to 40 ms" Safari landmark estimate has no source; the only published browser numbers are 5 to 15 fps on an iPhone 11 | Plan for 60 to 150 ms per analysed frame on a 2021-era iPhone, 30 to 60 ms on a 2024-era one. Split L134's rate rule: capture decimation into the ring buffer is a hard 10 fps in Escalating/Active; the landmark rate is measured, one frame in flight, drops counted; L180 becomes "hand busy in 3 consecutive analysed frames within 500 ms"; the packet gains `landmark_fps` |
| BL-11 (new) | Persisted 21-point landmarks for every hand, plus a stored wearer hand template, are a "scan of hand geometry" under Illinois BIPA and an enrollment under Washington's law | The packet keeps landmarks (the localizer needs them). After localization the server reduces the stored record to `hand_bbox`, `handedness`, per-frame `hand_present` and a wearer hand-size scalar; the raw landmark file has its own `retention_until` (72 h initial, 7-day ceiling). The wearer calibration is a per-session scalar plus a wearer-only appearance bank under a written release; `other_person` and `unknown` are the only non-wearer outputs, with no template for anyone else. During weeks 1 to 4 on the team's own consented footage, raw landmarks are retained for controller equivalence tests |

Two further replacements in the same freeze:

- **Reliable sighting (L189)** is restated on five conditions: the identity head's conformal prediction set has size 1 at the item's alpha (0.05; 0.01 for medication, where `confident` additionally requires an OCR identifier or a user name on the matched exemplar); `location_status` is `placed` or `sighted`; the crop passed the quality gate; no co-visibility contradiction (degrading to no-refusal-logged when the homography fails); and it is the item's most recent location hypothesis. The 0.15 margin survives only as the logged interim (`calibration_id = margin_0.15_initial`) until the CUTE and rig calibrations exist. Singleton = match; empty = new; two or more = ambiguous, no bank write, lookalike link.
- **Idempotency rule for re-sent packets**: same `episode_id` + same digest = no-op ack; same id + different digest = stored as a revision (superset recomputes localization; conflict keeps the first canonical), never an error to the phone and never an overwrite. Digest is over canonical JSON with JPEGs replaced by their hashes.

## 7. Commercial constraints the build must respect

- **FDA.** Object finding is general wellness as long as marketing names no disease and nothing computes, schedules, confirms or escalates a dose. "Memory impairment" as a description of the user population is tolerable; "treats", "manages" or "monitors" a condition is not. The spec's line "dose text never feeds a dosing feature" is what keeps PAM out of two device categories; `doses.py` stays out of the memory layer, and any future adherence feature is user-entered, never camera-inferred, with no alert to a professional.
- **HIPAA and FTC.** A Medicare-certified home-health agency as customer makes PAM a business associate: BAA with the agency and down the chain (Deepgram, the LLM provider, any labeling vendor). A family as customer triggers the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (a product that "tracks medications"). Two deployment profiles, `family` and `agency`, set at provisioning by the operator, immutable, checked at every egress call site, with a test asserting zero non-BAA egress under `agency`; the eventual agency build omits the non-BAA clients entirely.
- **Recording consent.** No ambient microphone, ever: audio exists only inside a push-to-talk clip and a caregiver's deliberate voice note. That invariant keeps the household out of all-party wiretap statutes. Video needs the visible indicator, a one-touch pause reachable by an aide, a posted disclosure and a signed acknowledgement from every regular entrant. First pilot households in one-party states or with signed all-party consent; no Massachusetts or Illinois pilot without counsel's sign-off.
- **Biometrics.** No face detection, face embedding or person re-identification anywhere, including the charger tier. Hand geometry per BL-11. No voiceprint: STT produces text; speaker identification is never added.
- **Caregiver as data subject.** The caregiver's identity and reports are stored and spoken back to the wearer by name; the caregiver needs their own consent (pilot) and terms (product), a withdrawal path (the `source` version bump), and both consents disclose that reports are repeated to the wearer.
- **Retention numbers counsel fixes before the IRB packet** (because they appear in a consent): raw landmarks 72 h with a 7-day ceiling; wearer calibration until offboarding, deleted within 30 days; audio clips 30 days; caregiver photos while the report is live plus 30 days; evidence keyframes while the item is durable or until offboarding; non-evidence frames 30 days; withdrawal within 30 days; training exports indefinite only under the separate global-training consent, which must name the exportable classes (crops, masks, reduced hand geometry, scrubbed OCR and transcripts) and the excluded ones (audio, caregiver identity, raw landmarks, calibration), and must say that pooled samples cannot be withdrawn and may transfer with the business.
- **Vendor terms that default to training on our users' data.** Deepgram (`mip_opt_out=true` on every request); Roboflow private projects (written opt-out or Enterprise before the first upload); Edge Impulse (do not use). Keep a one-page vendor data-flow table in the repo.
- **The name.** "PAM" is not clear as a mark for health-adjacent software: a live registration (Insignia Health, patient health-behavior software, Class 42), a pending AI-assistant application filed August 2025, and Conagra's famous mark. Keep "pam" as the internal codename; choose and clear a product mark before any public pilot material; user-facing strings take the name from config.
- **What an acquirer's counsel flags first**, in order: the AGPL and research-only artifacts in the tree (one day to fix); non-commercial footage in fixtures (half a day, or £7,000); the biometric surface (two days of spec and schema); the consent and bystander plan as a product feature (a week, mostly writing); vendor data-flow defaults (one day); the mark (an afternoon plus fees); the regulatory claims statement (two days); the HIPAA/FTC posture (a week of counsel time).
